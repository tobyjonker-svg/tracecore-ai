# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** March 31, 2026

TraceCore AI is committed to providing exceptional value and service to our customers. This policy outlines how we handle refunds, cancellations, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 1. Subscription Cancellation

### 1.1 Cancellation Process

You can cancel your TraceCore AI subscription at any time through your account settings. No questions asked, no cancellation fees. To cancel:

1. Log into your TraceCore AI account
2. Navigate to **Settings → Billing → Subscription**
3. Click **Cancel Subscription**
4. Confirm your cancellation

Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period. You will not be charged for the next billing cycle.

### 1.2 Immediate Cancellation

If you need immediate cancellation before your billing period ends, contact our support team at support@tracecoreai.com. We will process your request within 24 hours.

---

## 2. Refund Policy

### 2.1 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind our product. If you're not satisfied with TraceCore AI within the first **30 days** of your subscription, we'll refund 100% of your payment—no questions asked.

**Eligibility:**
- Applies to new subscriptions only (first 30 days from payment date)
- Applies to Pro and Pro Plus tiers
- Free tier is not eligible for refunds (it's free)

**How to Request:**
Email support@tracecoreai.com with:
- Your account email
- Reason for refund request
- Proof of payment (if available)

We'll process your refund within 5 business days.

### 2.2 Refunds After 30 Days

After the initial 30-day period, we do not offer refunds for subscription fees. However, we're committed to your success. If you're experiencing issues, please contact our support team before canceling. We may be able to:

- Adjust your plan to better fit your needs
- Provide training or onboarding support
- Troubleshoot technical issues
- Offer a temporary discount or pause

### 2.3 Prorated Refunds for Plan Changes

If you upgrade or downgrade your subscription mid-billing cycle:

- **Upgrades:** You'll be charged the difference immediately. No refund.
- **Downgrades:** You'll receive a prorated credit toward your next billing cycle.

**Example:** If you downgrade from Pro Plus (R299/month) to Pro (R199/month) on day 15 of your billing cycle:
- Credit = (R299 - R199) × (15 days remaining ÷ 30 days in month) = R50 credit

## 3. Billing & Payment Issues

### 3.1 Failed Payments

If your payment fails:

1. We'll send you an email notification
2. You have **7 days** to update your payment method
3. If payment is not received, your subscription will be suspended
4. Your data remains safe and accessible if you update payment within 30 days
5. After 30 days, your account and data may be deleted

### 3.2 Duplicate Charges

If you're charged twice by mistake, contact support@tracecoreai.com immediately with:
- Your account email
- Screenshots of duplicate charges
- Transaction IDs (if available)

We'll investigate and issue a refund within 5 business days.

### 3.3 Billing Disputes

For billing disputes or chargebacks:

1. Contact us first at support@tracecoreai.com
2. Provide details of the dispute
3. We'll work with you to resolve the issue
4. If unresolved, disputes may be escalated to your payment processor

**Note:** Chargebacks without prior communication may result in account suspension.

---

## 4. Data & Account Closure

### 4.1 Data Export Before Cancellation

Before canceling, you can export all your data:

1. Go to **Settings → Data & Privacy → Export Data**
2. Select data types (Products, Orders, Suppliers, Reports, etc.)
3. Download as CSV or JSON
4. Export is available for 30 days after cancellation

### 4.2 Data Retention After Cancellation

- **First 30 days:** Your account and data are preserved. You can reactivate anytime.
- **After 30 days:** Your account will be marked for deletion.
- **After 90 days:** Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

You can request immediate deletion by emailing support@tracecoreai.com.

### 4.3 Reactivation

If you cancel and change your mind within 30 days, you can reactivate your subscription:

1. Log in to your account (if still accessible)
2. Click **Reactivate Subscription**
3. Choose your plan
4. Your previous data will be restored
